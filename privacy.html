RKC Bot - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how RKC, a Discord bot, may process and store information when users interact with its features.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features implemented and enabled, RKC may process or store:

  • Discord user IDs and server IDs.
  • Virtual economy balances, inventories and transactions.
  • Experience points (XP), levels and activity statistics.
  • Moderation warnings, reasons, moderator IDs and timestamps.
  • Server settings and bot preferences.
  • Technical error logs and diagnostic information.

RKC is intended to avoid storing message contents unless a feature requires it and that practice is disclosed.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to operate bot commands, maintain game progress and settings, provide moderation features, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical problems.

4. Storage and Security

Depending on the final implementation, information may be stored in a database or processed by hosting providers. Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect stored data. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the feature or purpose for which it is used. Users may request deletion of eligible stored information through the RKC support channel. Some records may need to be retained for security or legal reasons.

Actual retention periods and deletion procedures depend on the features and systems implemented by the bot owner.

6. Sharing of Information

RKC does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by providers required to host and operate the bot, or disclosed when required by law.

7. Children's Privacy

Users must meet Discord's applicable age requirements and comply with applicable laws and Discord's terms.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the RKC owner through the official RKC support channel.
